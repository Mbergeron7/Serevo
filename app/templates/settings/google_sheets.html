Setup Guide

1. Create a Service Account
Go to the Google Cloud Console, create a service account, and download the JSON key file.

2. Enable the Sheets API
In the same project, enable the Google Sheets API and the Google Drive API.

3. Share Your Sheet
Open your Google Sheet, click Share, and add the service account email (found as client_email in the JSON) with Viewer access.

4. Required Tabs
Your sheet should have these tabs: EMPLOYEES, FORECAST RAW, REQUIREMENTS RAW, and optionally PlanningUnits.
