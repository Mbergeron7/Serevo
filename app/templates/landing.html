Everything you need for WFM

From forecasting to agent empowerment, {{ brand_name }} covers the full workforce management lifecycle.

📈

Demand Forecasting

Generate accurate forecasts at 15-minute intervals using multiple methods — moving average, weighted, exponential smoothing, and ensemble. Track accuracy and reforecast on the fly.

📅

Smart Scheduling

Auto-generate optimized schedules based on demand, availability, skills, and labor rules. Visual timeline with drag-to-adjust segments, coverage heatmaps, and gap analysis.

📡

Real-Time Management

12 live monitoring tabs: intraday coverage, agent status, adherence, service level estimates (Erlang C), net staffing, absenteeism, efficiency, and more — all auto-refreshing.

👥

Capacity Planning

Plan headcount needs months ahead with capacity models driven by your actual volume and handle-time trends. Long-range and short-range views.

📋

Attendance Tracking

Auto-detect absences, lates, and early leaves by comparing schedules to actual logins. Manual coding with exception codes. Badges show directly on the schedule.

🔑

Agent Portal

Self-service portal where agents view schedules, clock in/out, request PTO, bid on shifts, and swap with teammates — reducing supervisor overhead.

⭐

Quality Management

Score evaluations, track coaching sessions, and measure agent performance alongside scheduling and adherence data.

📊

Reports & Payroll

Export schedules, attendance summaries, and hours worked. Payroll-ready views with clock punches and overtime calculations.

🎯

WFM Ticketing

Track schedule changes, overtime requests, and exceptions with a built-in WFM ticketing system for team leads and supervisors.
