Getting Started

▸

First-Time Setup

When you first access {{ brand_name }}, you'll see the setup page if no admin account exists yet. Create your admin account by entering your email and a strong password. This becomes the primary administrator account.

Logging In

Navigate to your {{ brand_name }} URL and enter your email and password. If your organization has Google or Microsoft sign-in enabled, you can use the social login buttons instead.

Sessions last 8 hours. After that, you'll need to sign in again.

Navigation

The top navigation bar shows all available sections based on your role. Admins see all sections. Supervisors see most sections. Agents see only their personal portal (My Schedule, My Time Off, Self-Service).

Use the search bar (or press Ctrl+K / ⌘K) to quickly find employees, pages, or actions.

Notifications

Click the bell icon in the top right to see your notifications. These include PTO requests, schedule changes, and system alerts. Notifications are automatically marked as read when you open them.

Install as an App

{{ brand_name }} can be installed on your phone or computer for quick access — no app store needed.

Android (Chrome): Tap the three-dot menu and select Add to Home Screen. You'll get an app icon that opens {{ brand_name }} in its own window.

iPhone / iPad (Safari): Tap the share button (square with arrow), then tap Add to Home Screen.

Desktop (Chrome): Look for the install icon in the right side of the address bar and click it to pin {{ brand_name }} as a desktop app.

User Roles & Permissions

▸

Admin

Full access to all features: manage users, configure settings, view all data, generate reports, manage schedules, handle approvals, and access the audit log. Admins can create and manage other user accounts.

Supervisor

View access to most management sections (Dashboard, People, Scheduling, Forecasting, Capacity, Real-Time, Attendance, Quality, Approvals, Reports, Payroll, Shift Notes, Documents, Training, Announcements). Supervisors can also access the agent portal for their own schedule. Some edit operations may be restricted compared to admins.

Agent

Access is limited to the personal portal: My Schedule (view assigned shifts), My Time Off (request PTO), and Self-Service (update personal info, view announcements, clock in/out). Agents cannot access management pages.

Managing Users

Go to Settings → Users to add, edit, or deactivate user accounts. When creating a user, choose the appropriate role. You can link a user account to an employee record so agents can see their own schedule and data.

Dashboard

▸

The Dashboard is the home page for admins and supervisors. It shows at-a-glance metrics for your workforce.

KPI Cards

The top row displays key metrics: total active employees, employees scheduled today, pending PTO requests, open shifts, and more. These update in real time as data changes.

Charts

Below the KPI cards, you'll find charts showing headcount trends, attendance rates, and other workforce analytics over time. Hover over data points for details.

Recent Activity

The activity feed shows the latest changes across the system — new hires, schedule updates, approved PTO, and other events.

People (Roster)

▸

Viewing the Roster

The People page shows all employees in a sortable, filterable table. Use the filters at the top to narrow by name, line of business (LOB), status, or team lead. Click column headers to sort.

Pagination

Choose how many employees to show per page (25, 50, 100, or All) using the page size selector. Use the Previous/Next buttons to navigate through pages.

Adding an Employee

Click + Add Employee. Fill in at least the first name, last name, employee ID, and status. You can also set contract type, weekly hours, skills, LOB, team lead, and more. Click Save.

Editing an Employee

Click on any employee row to open their profile. Click the edit button to modify their details. Changes are saved immediately.

Employee Profile

Each employee has a profile page showing their details, schedule history, PTO history, documents, training assignments, and accommodations.

PTO & Accommodations

Access PTO management and accommodations tracking from the People submenu. You can view, add, and manage time-off entries and accommodation records for each employee.

Scheduling

▸

Viewing Schedules

The Scheduling page displays employee schedules in a calendar view. Switch between daily and weekly views. Filter by LOB or team lead to focus on specific groups.

Creating Schedules

Click on empty cells to create new shift assignments. Select the employee, choose a day model (shift template), and set the date. You can create individual shifts or bulk-generate schedules.

Schedule Generation

Use the "Generate" feature to automatically create schedules based on demand forecasts, employee availability, skills, and contract rules. Review the generated schedule before publishing.

Publishing

Generated schedules start in draft mode. Click "Publish" to make them visible to agents. Published schedules appear in each agent's My Schedule portal.

Day Models

Day models define shift templates (start time, end time, break duration). Set these up in Settings → Day Models before creating schedules.

Forecasting

▸

Overview

The Forecasting module helps predict future workload demand. Upload historical volume data to generate forecasts that feed into capacity planning and schedule generation.

Viewing Forecasts

Select a planning unit (LOB) and date range to view forecast data. Charts show predicted vs. actual volumes by interval.

Importing Data

Use the Data Import section to upload historical contact volumes. The system uses this data to calculate trends and generate forecasts.

Capacity Planning

▸

Capacity Panel

The Capacity Panel shows current staffing levels against requirements. View by planning unit to see where you're overstaffed or understaffed.

Capacity Summary

The summary view aggregates capacity data across all planning units, showing overall staffing health and trends.

Long-Range Planning

Use long-range planning to project headcount needs weeks or months ahead. This connects to your forecast data to estimate future staffing requirements.

Google Sheets Integration

Capacity data can be pulled from connected Google Sheets. Set up the integration in Settings → Google Sheets by entering your sheet key and ensuring the service account has access.

Real-Time Management

▸

Real-Time Dashboard

Monitor workforce activity in real time. See who's logged in, on break, or off-schedule. Track adherence to scheduled activities.

VTO / OT Management

Manage voluntary time off (VTO) and overtime (OT) offers. When volumes are lower than expected, offer VTO. When volumes spike, offer OT to available agents.

Attendance

▸

Time Clock

Employees can clock in and out from the agent portal. Admins and supervisors can view all clock punches and make corrections.

Attendance Records

View attendance data by date range. See who was absent, late, or left early. Filter by employee, date, or status.

Editing Punches

Admins can edit clock punches to correct errors. All edits are tracked in the audit log with the original and corrected times.

Quality

▸

Quality Scores

Track quality evaluation scores for each employee. Add scores by category (customer satisfaction, accuracy, professionalism, etc.).

Quality Reviews

Create quality review entries with scores, notes, and feedback. Link reviews to specific dates and employees for tracking trends over time.

Approvals

▸

Pending Requests

The Approvals page shows all pending PTO and time-off requests. Review each request to see the employee, dates, type, and any conflicts with the schedule.

Approving / Denying

Click on a request to review details. Choose to approve or deny. Add an optional note explaining the decision. The employee is notified of the outcome.

History

View past approvals and denials. Filter by date range, employee, or status to review decision history.

Reports

▸

Summary Reports

View KPI summaries showing active employees, average hours, PTO usage, and other workforce metrics over a selected time period.

Attendance Reports

Detailed attendance data showing clock-in/out times, total hours, absences, and tardiness for each employee.

CSV Export

Click the export button to download any report as a CSV file. Choose between summary and attendance exports. The CSV includes all visible data and can be opened in Excel or Google Sheets.

Payroll

▸

Payroll Overview

The Payroll section aggregates scheduled hours, overtime, and time-off data for each pay period. Use this to prepare payroll submissions.

Pay Period Selection

Select the pay period dates to calculate total hours for each employee. The system automatically accounts for scheduled shifts, clock punches, and approved time off.

Training

▸

Training Modules

Create training modules with a title, description, category, and estimated duration. Mark modules as required if they're mandatory for all employees.

Assigning Training

Assign modules to individual employees or bulk-assign to groups. Set a due date to track completion timelines.

Tracking Progress

View completion status for all assignments. Filter by status (assigned, in progress, completed, overdue) to identify who needs follow-up.

Other Sections

▸

Shift Notes

Leave notes for the next shift. Supervisors and admins can post notes that are visible to incoming staff. Useful for handoffs and important updates.

Employee Documents

Upload and manage documents for each employee (contracts, certifications, ID copies). Documents are stored securely and linked to employee profiles.

Announcements

Post announcements visible to all agents on their portal. Pin important announcements to keep them at the top. Set expiration dates for time-limited notices.

Team Calendar

A shared calendar view showing team schedules, PTO, and events. Useful for seeing coverage at a glance.

Data Import

Import employee data, schedules, and contact volumes from CSV files or connected systems. Use the manual entry form for individual records.

Audit Log

Admin-only. View a chronological record of all significant actions: user logins, data changes, schedule publications, and configuration updates. Filter by action type, entity, or date range.

Settings

▸

General / Customization

Set your organization's brand name, accent color, tagline, and support email. These appear throughout the application.

Users

Add, edit, and manage user accounts. Set roles (admin, supervisor, agent) and link users to employee records. Deactivate accounts when people leave.

Planning Units

Define your lines of business (LOBs) or workload groups. Planning units are used throughout scheduling, forecasting, and capacity planning.

Day Models

Create shift templates defining start time, end time, and break duration. Day models are assigned to employees when building schedules.

Contracts

Define contract types (Full-Time, Part-Time, etc.) with weekly hour targets. Contracts help enforce scheduling rules.

Skills

Manage the skills inventory. Assign skills to employees for skill-based scheduling and routing.

Activities

Define activities that employees perform (calls, emails, chat, training, meetings). Used in scheduling and real-time adherence.

Google Sheets Integration

Connect Google Sheets for capacity data. Enter the sheet key and share the sheet with the service account email shown on the settings page.

API Connections

Configure connections to external systems for data import. Set up API keys and endpoints for automated data feeds.

OAuth / Social Login

Enable Google and/or Microsoft sign-in for your users. Set up OAuth client credentials in Settings → API Connections.

Agent Portal (Self-Service)

▸

My Schedule

View your upcoming shifts in a weekly calendar. See start/end times, activities, and any special notes for each shift.

My Time Off

Request time off by selecting dates and a reason. View the status of your requests (pending, approved, denied). See your remaining PTO balance.

Self-Service Portal

View announcements, clock in/out for your shift, and access personal information. The portal is your one-stop for everything you need as an agent.

Clock In / Clock Out

Use the time clock button to record your start and end times. You can add notes to your punches (e.g., "arrived early for training").

Support & Troubleshooting

▸

Submitting a Support Ticket

Go to Support Tickets from the navigation menu. Click "New Ticket" and fill in the subject, description, category, and priority. Your ticket will be reviewed by an admin.

Tracking Your Tickets

View the status of your submitted tickets on the Support page. You can filter by status and category. Click a ticket to see its details and any admin responses.

Common Issues

Can't log in: Make sure you're using the correct email address. If you've forgotten your password, contact your admin to reset it.

Missing data: Check that data has been imported for the date range you're viewing. Go to Data Import to verify.

Schedule not showing: Schedules must be published before agents can see them. Ask your supervisor if schedules have been published for your dates.

Permission denied: You may not have the required role for that action. Contact your admin if you need elevated access.
